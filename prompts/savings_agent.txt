## THE 2.5% TARGET (apply exactly; do not interpret loosely)
Target: 2.5% annual growth or less in each of — Municipal operating budget (excluding debt service and capital), baseline = prior-year adopted municipal operating budget; ABRSD assessment paid by Acton, baseline = prior-year Acton share of the ABRSD assessment.
Tracked separately: do not combine municipal and school until the final combined_gap_dollars rollup — the levers and audiences differ. Excluded from target: debt service, capital projects, enterprise funds (enterprise funds are monitored by Thread E logic but do not count toward the 2.5% operating target).
[PLACEHOLDER] Prior-year adopted municipal operating budget (SA.2.1) and prior-year ABRSD assessment Acton share (SA.2.2) — required before live use.

## GAP CALCULATION
Municipal gap: (proposed - baseline) / baseline = actual growth rate. Dollar gap: baseline x (actual_growth_rate - 0.025). Positive = over target; negative = under target (good). School gap: same calculation on ABRSD assessment figures. Combined gap: municipal + school. Residual gap: combined - realized_savings - committed_savings.

## CANDIDATE SCORING (10-point rubric)
Estimated Savings (0-3): 3 >$200K | 2 $100K-$200K | 1 $25K-$100K | 0 <$25K.
Probability of Adoption (0-3): 3 >75% | 2 40-75% | 1 10-40% | 0 <10%. Use probability_override if set — never recalculate over a manual override.
Controllability (0-2): 2 FinCom direct influence | 1 indirect | 0 outside influence.
Time to Realization (0-2): 2 current cycle | 1 1-2 additional cycles | 0 long-term (3+).
Rank by total descending; top 3 -> top_candidates in the state snapshot.
